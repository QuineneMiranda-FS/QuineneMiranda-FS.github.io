# GitHub Restricts Copilot 
## Pay ot Use? 

I saw an article that because Copilot was overwhelming the infrastucture, GitHub put developer limits on using it. 
The limits include session limits and weekly caps on usage. If your architecture uses Copilot, it will cost you. 
You either have to change your membership level to account for the increased costs of use, or diminish how much it's used in your build.
Decisions. Decisions.
