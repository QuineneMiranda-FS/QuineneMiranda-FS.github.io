# Oracle
### Concerning?

I heard recently that Oracle did another round of layoffs. I'm rather disturbed about that. We only had that one class, but I found databases really relaxing. I know that is likely not a common response/feeling in regards to databases. I know. 

But I had plans to learn more database programs and skills in hopes of working towards a database position. Now, I'm thinking there may not be positions I could even apprentice into - especially since the highly skilled and experienced have been given the boot.

Anyhow, if you haven't heard this is the summary of positions affected:

* Development: Software Engineers, Developers, Site Reliability Engineers, Technical Analysts, User Experience Developers. 
* Management: Product Managers, Program Managers, Product Developers, Senior Directors, Vice Presidents. 
* Support & Operations: Customer Success Teams, QA Analysts.
